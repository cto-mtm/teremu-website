## How to calculate the real cost of a delivery dish

In a ghost kitchen, food cost is measured against what you keep, not what the customer pays. Do it in three steps:

1. **Cost the dish.** Add up each ingredient (amount used × the price per pound or piece you paid) and add the packaging: clamshell, bag, sauce cup, utensils.
2. **Net revenue per order.** Subtract the app's fee from the menu price. A $14 dish with a 25% fee leaves you $10.50.
3. **Real percentage.** Divide the cost by the net revenue. A $4.50 cost on $10.50 is 42.9%, even though against the menu price it looks like 32%.

That number tells you how to price: if you want cost to be 40% of what you receive, divide the cost by 0.40 to get the net revenue you need ($4.50 ÷ 0.40 = $11.25), then divide that by (1 − fee): $11.25 ÷ 0.75 = $15.00 on the app. The full worked example is in our guide to [ghost kitchen delivery app fees](/en/blog/ghost-kitchen-delivery-app-fees).

## Why the spreadsheet falls behind

Doing the math once is easy. Redoing it every time a price changes is not. In a kitchen running several brands, a chicken price increase hits wings, bowls and sandwiches at once, and nobody recosts twenty dishes on a Tuesday night.

Teremu ties that work to something you already do: receiving invoices. Every time you scan one, the new price flows into every dish that uses that ingredient, and you immediately see which ones fell below your target margin.

## What to watch in a ghost kitchen

- **Proteins and fryer oil**: usually the most expensive items and the ones whose prices move most.
- **Packaging**: every delivery dish ships with its container and bag. Include them in each dish's cost.
- **In-app promotions**: a discount you fund lowers your net revenue, and your cost percentage rises even though nothing changed in the kitchen.
- **Low-price items**: once the fee is taken, a cheap side can leave you almost nothing. Check those first.
- **Invoice discrepancies**: Teremu matches the delivery note against the invoice to catch charges that don't belong.
