# ChatGPT or Teremu for your food business's costs?

**Short answer:** a general chat assistant like ChatGPT is very good at one-off jobs: explaining a formula, running the numbers you paste in, or writing a dish description. But it works from what you give it in each conversation, doesn't keep a running record of your supplier prices, and doesn't alert you when something changes. Teremu does: it reads your invoices, keeps every recipe cost current and alerts you. For most food businesses, the answer is to use each one for what it's good at.

## What a general chat assistant is good at

Let's be clear: these tools are useful, and plenty of people in kitchens already use them well.

- **Explaining concepts.** What food cost is, how to work out a dish's margin, what the 28–35% band means.
- **One-off math.** Paste a recipe's ingredients and prices and get a cost per portion.
- **Writing.** Menu descriptions, an email to a vendor, a social post.
- **Thinking out loud.** Ideas for a new dish, how to use up a leftover ingredient, how to organize a shift.

If you need a quick answer to a general question, a chat assistant is a great choice.

## Where it falls short for tracking your costs

A recipe's cost changes every time one of its ingredient prices changes. Tracking costs isn't a calculation; it's **a record that stays alive**. That's where a general assistant has natural limits:

- **It works from what you paste or upload each time.** For an updated cost, you have to give it your prices and recipes again.
- **It doesn't keep a history of your vendors.** It doesn't know what each vendor charged you last month unless you tell it.
- **It doesn't alert you.** If cream went up this week, nobody tells you until you ask, and only if you hand it the new invoice.
- **It doesn't link an ingredient to all your recipes.** Every calculation starts from scratch.
- **You check every number.** Math done from pasted text can carry a unit mix-up or a misread price, with no fixed check behind it.

None of this is a flaw. A general-purpose tool just isn't built to be your kitchen's back office.

## What Teremu does

Teremu is built for one job: knowing what every dish costs you today, without manual data entry.

- **Scan invoices with your phone:** paper, crumpled or PDF. AI extracts the vendor, date and every line, and you review it in Triage before it counts.
- **Prices roll into every recipe.** When an ingredient's price changes, every recipe that uses it recalculates itself.
- **Target vs actual margin** on every dish.
- **In-app alerts** when a price goes up or a dish drops below its margin.
- **Pulse**, the dashboard, shows your food cost % against the 28–35% band.
- **Theoretical pantry, shopping list and a vendor directory** that builds itself.
- **Kitchen Assistant** (Pro and Max): ask in plain language, for example what went up in price this week, and it answers over your account's data, filtered by each team member's permissions.

It runs on iOS, Android and the web, and you don't need a POS system.

## Which should you pick?

**Use only a general chat assistant if:**

- You cost your recipes now and then, not every week.
- Your prices barely change.
- You don't mind pasting your data in every time.

**Use Teremu if:**

- You get invoices every week and prices move.
- You want each dish's real margin today, not last month's.
- You want alerts without having to ask.
- You want your team to scan invoices without seeing what they shouldn't.

**Use both** if you want the best of each: the general chat assistant for writing and thinking, Teremu for your numbers.
