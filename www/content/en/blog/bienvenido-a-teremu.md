# Welcome to Teremu

Teremu turns your phone camera into your restaurant's back office. Scan crumpled vendor invoices and AI keeps your food costs, dish margins, inventory, and supplier prices up to date — without any manual data entry.

## The idea

Our philosophy is simple: **smart approximation instead of bookkeeping**. Teremu never asks you to log anything daily. It builds an accurate, always-current picture from what already exists — the papers your suppliers hand you and the sales you ring up — and lets a human correct only at the moments that matter.

## What's next

On this blog we'll share insights on food cost, recipe costing, supplier negotiation, and how independent restaurants recover margin points week after week. Welcome aboard.
