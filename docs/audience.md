# Who the site speaks to

Internal copywriting brief. Never publish it on the site. The full persona, with sources, lives in the app repo: `teremu-app/docs/persona-owner-chef.md`. That file is canonical; this one is the short version for copy.

## The reader

**The owner-chef who does it all.** An experienced, passionate cook who now owns one small food business (a counter shop, a food truck, a bakery or café) and is personally the chef, the cashier, the buyer, the marketer and the bookkeeper.

- Grew up in kitchens: a family restaurant, serious kitchens in big cities, or a restaurant of their own before this one. Not a novice at food. Out of hours for the back office.
- Buys from a distributor *and* on cash-and-carry runs (Restaurant Depot, Costco). Much of what they buy comes with a paper receipt, not an invoice.
- Often has side revenue: packaged or wholesale items for coffee shops, catering, events.
- Reads the site on a phone, between lunch and dinner, or at night after doing their Instagram on Canva.
- Has tried costing the menu in a spreadsheet, and the spreadsheet is months out of date. Has tried inventory counts and quit.

The same copy must still work for a larger independent (several staff, a chef who isn't the owner). Write for the owner-chef first.

## What to lead with

1. **The dish:** "what this dish really costs you today", not food-cost percentages.
2. **Time:** no typing receipts, no Sunday-night spreadsheet, a few minutes after each supply run.
3. **Setup in minutes:** find your restaurant on Google, the menu comes in, recipes are drafted for you. No consultant, no training.
4. **Money**, framed as an estimate: about 1–2% of food purchases ($100–200 a month on $10k).
5. **Free, honestly:** free to start, no card, with clear caps (25 invoices a month, 90 days of history, owner only). Pro $39 when they want their team and every invoice.

## Words

| Use | Avoid |
|---|---|
| what this dish really costs today | COGS, plate-cost variance |
| your prices went up | vendor price creep, PPI |
| snap the receipt or the invoice | ingest, OCR, documents |
| it builds your order | par levels, theoretical inventory |
| you don't have to count anything | true-up, reconciliation |
| free, no card | freemium, tiers, SaaS |

Spanish stays the source of truth for site copy (`CLAUDE.md`). The same plain register applies in both languages: say "lo que te cuesta este plato hoy", not "coste de materia prima".

## Don't promise

- Toast sales import: say "coming" only where needed. Square connects today; Toast is waiting on Toast's approval.
- Gmail invoice import, WhatsApp share-to-scan, bank feeds: built or designed, not live for customers.
- Warehouse-club receipts (Restaurant Depot, Costco) reading cleanly: untested until verified with real receipts.
- Measured savings: there are no customer results yet. Every money figure is an estimate and says so.
